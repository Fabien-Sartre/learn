# Finance glossary

Seed glossary for the finance track. Definitions should be refined during lessons.

| Term | Working definition |
|---|---|
| Accounting entity | The organization whose financial activity and position are being recorded separately from owners and other parties. |
| Company | An organization that conducts economic activity through a separate legal/accounting boundary. |
| Economic activity | Transactions and events such as selling, buying, employing, producing, investing, borrowing, and paying. |
| Financial position | What a company controls and owes at a point in time, plus the owners’ residual claim. |
| Performance | How the company did over a period, usually described through income, expenses, and profit or loss. |
| Asset | A present economic resource controlled by the company that is expected to provide future economic benefit. |
| Liability | A present obligation of the company to transfer an economic resource. |
| Equity | The residual interest in the company after liabilities are subtracted from assets. |
| Accounting equation | Assets = liabilities + equity. |
| Revenue | Income from the company’s ordinary activities, such as selling goods or services. It is not necessarily cash received. |
| Income | Increases in economic benefits that increase equity, other than owner contributions; revenue is ordinary-activity income. |
| Expense | Decreases in economic benefits that reduce equity, other than distributions to owners. It is not necessarily cash paid. |
| Profit / loss | Income minus expenses over a period. Profit is positive; loss is negative. |
| Cash | Money and cash equivalents available to or moving through the business. |
| Cash flow | Cash inflows and outflows during a period. |
| Liquidity | Ability to meet cash obligations when due. |
| Balance sheet / statement of financial position | Statement showing assets, liabilities, and equity at a point in time. |
| Income statement / statement of profit or loss | Statement showing income, expenses, and profit or loss over a period. |
| Cash flow statement | Statement showing cash inflows and outflows over a period, commonly grouped into operating, investing, and financing activities. |
| Accrual accounting | Accounting that recognizes economic events when they occur, not only when cash moves. |
| Receivable | Amount owed to the company, typically by customers. |
| Payable | Amount the company owes to suppliers or other parties. |
| Inventory | Goods held for sale or used in production. |
| Working capital | Short-term operating resources and obligations; commonly current assets minus current liabilities. |
| Capital expenditure / CapEx | Spending to acquire or improve long-term assets. |
| Operating expenditure / OpEx | Spending for ongoing operations, usually recognized as operating expenses when incurred or consumed. |
| Depreciation | Systematic allocation of the cost of a tangible long-term asset over its useful life. |
| Amortization | Systematic allocation of the cost of an intangible asset over its useful life. |
| Debt | Borrowed money or financing obligation that must be repaid under agreed terms. |
| Retained earnings | Accumulated profits kept in the business after losses and distributions/dividends. |
| Gross profit | Revenue minus cost of goods sold/cost of sales. |
| Operating profit | Profit from operations before financing and tax effects, subject to the reporting framework and presentation. |
| Net profit | Profit after all recognized income and expenses, including tax, for the period. |
| EBITDA | Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization; a non-GAAP/non-IFRS performance measure often used as a proxy for operating earnings before certain costs. |
| Budget | A financial plan for a future period, often approved as a target. |
| Forecast | An updated estimate of future financial results based on current expectations. |
| Actuals | Financial results that have occurred and been recorded. |
| Variance | Difference between actuals and a comparison point such as budget, forecast, or prior period. |
